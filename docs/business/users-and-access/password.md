# Change or Reset Your Password

You change your own password inside the Ottu Dashboard. If you forgot it and cannot sign in, an administrator of your account sets a new one for you.

## How do I change my password?

**Who can do it:** any user who is signed in.

1. In the Ottu Dashboard, click the three-dot menu in the top-right corner, next to your name.
2. Click **Account settings**.
3. Click **Change password**.
4. Type the new password in **New password** and again in **Confirm password**.
5. Click **Submit**.

The message **Password changed successfully** appears. Use the new password the next time you sign in.

If you sign in with your company account (single sign-on), you have no Ottu password to change: change it at your company's sign-in provider.

## I forgot my password

Ask an administrator of your Ottu account to set a new password for you (steps below). When you are signed in again, change it to one only you know, as described above.

## Reset the password of another user

**Who can do it:** an administrator of your account.

1. In the Ottu Dashboard, click the three-dot menu in the top-right corner and click **Administration panel**.
2. In the top menu, click **Users**, then click the user.
3. In the password row, click **Reset password**.
4. Type the new password twice and confirm.
5. Give the new password to the user in a safe way, and ask them to change it after they sign in.

## Password rules

A new password must:

- have at least 8 characters;
- contain at least one digit, one uppercase letter and one special character, such as `!` or `#`;
- not be only digits, and not be a common password;
- not be too similar to your username, name or email;
- be different from your current password and your last 5 passwords.
