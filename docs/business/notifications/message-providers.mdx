# Message Providers

Ottu sends your [SMS](/business/notifications/sms) and [WhatsApp](/business/notifications/whatsapp/) notifications through a message provider: an SMS company or a WhatsApp business provider. The account with the provider is yours; Ottu connects to it.

## Which SMS or WhatsApp provider can I use?

Any provider in this catalogue. Switch between **SMS** and **WhatsApp**, and pick your **Country** to see the providers based there together with the international ones.

<ProviderCatalog />

A country is where the provider is based. International providers sell their service across many countries. For delivery to a specific country and for prices, ask the provider.

## How do I get a provider set up?

1. Pick a provider from the catalogue and open an account with it. The contract and the message fees are between you and the provider.
2. Contact [Ottu support](mailto:support@ottu.com) with the address of your Ottu Dashboard and the name of the provider.
3. Support tells you which details it needs from your provider account and how to hand them over safely.
4. Support connects the provider and confirms when it is active.

:::warning
Do not write a password, API key or token in an email or a chat message.
:::

## My provider is not on the list

Contact [Ottu support](mailto:support@ottu.com) with the provider's name and website. Support tells you whether it can be added.
