# AutoPay

Sell by subscription and let Ottu do the billing work. Your developer makes one checkout call. Ottu then generates every [billing cycle](/glossary/#term-billing-cycle), charges the saved card, retries failed charges, emails your customer, and hosts a page where they manage their own [subscription](/glossary/#term-subscription).

[**See it live**](/developers/payments/autopay/#live-demo): try the demo. It creates a test subscription on Ottu's sandbox.

## Why use AutoPay {#why-use-autopay}

- **Get paid on time without chasing.** Ottu charges the saved card every month or year. It retries failed payments on its own.
- **Keep customers informed without writing a word.** Ottu emails them reminders and failure notices, and texts them their page link, in English or Arabic.
- **Cut your support load.** Customers change their card, pay an overdue balance, see their history, or cancel on their own page.
- **Offer free trials without extra work.** Your customer saves a card and pays nothing today. Ottu charges the first real cycle on its own.

:::info Before you start
Your developer connects AutoPay through the API, on a [gateway with auto-debit](/business/payments/gateways/?features=autodebit). The full list is in [Limits](#limits).
:::

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Your team starts it, your customer pays once, and Ottu runs everything after. Click a step to see who does what. Building the integration? See [AutoPay for developers](/developers/payments/autopay/).

## What your customer sees {#what-your-customer-sees}

Your customer gets emails, text messages, and one private page. Ottu sends and hosts all of them.

### The emails and texts {#the-emails}

Ottu sends 6 emails and 3 texts, in English or Arabic, chosen when the subscription is created. Texts go to the customer's phone number when one is on file.

**Always sent.** You cannot switch these off:

- **Subscription created** (email + text). At once. Carries the first-payment link.
- **Setup completed** (email + text). Once, when the subscription leaves Pending setup.
- **Link replaced** (email + text). Each time your team regenerates the customer's page link.

**On by default.** You can switch these off, but only when the subscription is created:

- **Upcoming charge** (email). Monthly: 3 days before the charge. Yearly: 31, 11, and 4 days before. Skipped if a cancellation is pending.
- **Payment failed** (email). After each failed attempt while retries remain.
- **Final failure** (email). Right after the last attempt fails. See [When a payment fails](#when-a-payment-fails).

### The self-service page {#the-self-service-page}

Every subscription gets its own private page, hosted by Ottu and shown in English and Arabic. Ottu keeps it in sync with the subscription. The link is in every email Ottu sends. Treat it like a password: anyone holding the link can open the page.

From this page, a customer can:

- **See** their plan, status, and next charge date.
- **Review billing history**, including the next cycle, listed as **Scheduled** until it is charged.
- **Add a card**, or switch which saved card is active.
- **Pay** an outstanding balance.
- **Cancel**, or undo a pending cancellation.

:::note A customer's cancellation waits for the end of the paid period
It never takes effect immediately. Only your team can cancel immediately, through the API. Undo is available while the subscription is Active or Trialing and the cancellation is pending.

A Past Due customer cannot cancel. See [When a payment fails](#when-a-payment-fails).
:::

Customers can hold several cards and switch the active one in a click. Old cards stay on file, so the history always shows which card paid each cycle.

<a id="regenerate-link"></a>

:::warning Regenerating a link revokes the old one
The previous link stops working at once, and the page shows "This link has expired". Ottu emails and texts the customer the new link (see [Link replaced](#the-emails)). Links never expire on their own, so only a regeneration revokes one.
:::

## When a payment fails {#when-a-payment-fails}

- **Four attempts by default.** That is one initial charge plus three retries, about an hour apart. Your developer can set from 1 to 10 retries when they create the subscription. Ottu fixes the interval.
- **Your customer is emailed** after each failed attempt while retries remain, so they can update their card.
- **After the last attempt,** the subscription becomes [Past Due](#state-past-due) and your customer gets the final email with a link to pay. That email asks them to pay "to avoid suspension". Ottu never suspends anything. Whether your customer keeps access is your decision.
- **When they pay,** the subscription returns to Active.
- **A Past Due customer cannot cancel.** Ottu refuses the request until they pay the balance.

:::warning Ottu never cancels a Past Due subscription on its own
It stays Past Due until the customer pays or your team cancels it. You decide how long a Past Due customer keeps access. Your developer can list Past Due subscriptions through the API.
:::

## Lifecycle and billing cycles {#lifecycle-and-billing-cycles}

A subscription moves through seven states. Canceled and Expired are final. Click a state to read what it means and what you do.

<AutoPayStates audience="business" />

### Pending setup {#state-pending-setup}

Ottu created the subscription and waits for the first payment. Ottu activates it automatically after your customer pays. Your part: nothing.

### Trialing {#state-trialing}

The first payment was zero, so no money has moved. Ottu charges the saved card at the next billing cycle. Your part: nothing.

:::tip A zero first charge starts a trial
If the first charge is zero, the subscription starts as Trialing, not Active. The customer only saves a card. Canceling before the trial ends means no charge at all.
:::

### Active {#state-active}

Billing normally. The latest cycle was paid. Your part: give your customer the service.

### Past Due {#state-past-due}

Every retry on a cycle failed. The subscription waits for your customer to pay. Your part: decide what to do if it stays Past Due. See [When a payment fails](#when-a-payment-fails).

### Canceled {#state-canceled}

Billing has stopped. A cancellation set for the end of the period, by your customer or your team, lands here when that period ends. An immediate cancel by your team lands here at once. Your part: nothing.

### Expired {#state-expired}

The last cycle was paid and the next billing date would fall after the end date. Billing stopped. The history stays readable. To continue, create a new subscription.

### Setup Failed {#state-setup-failed}

The customer canceled the checkout, did not pay within 24 hours, or paid but the card could not be saved. Ottu does not retry it. Your part: create a new subscription.

### Billing cycles {#billing-cycles}

Ottu creates [billing cycles](/glossary/#term-billing-cycle) one at a time. The next cycle exists only once the current one is paid.

:::note A cycle's amount is locked when it is generated
Past cycles stay a reliable record of what Ottu charged.
:::

### Anchor days {#anchor-days}

Every subscription bills on the same day, called its anchor day. The start date sets it. A subscription anchored on the 29th, 30th, or 31st moves to the last day of a shorter month, then returns to its anchor day.

**Anchored on the 31st:** Jan 31 → Feb 28 (Feb 29 in a leap year) → Mar 31

## Setting up AutoPay {#setting-up-autopay}

Your developers call the API to create, look up, and cancel subscriptions. Before your first subscription, make sure you have:

1. **AutoPay enabled for your account.** Ask Ottu support to turn it on.
2. **A support email.** It appears on the customer's page and in the footer of every email Ottu sends.
3. **A privacy policy URL.** It is linked from the footer of every email Ottu sends.
4. **A payment gateway with auto-debit,** in the currency you bill in.
5. **The email and text templates registered,** in English and Arabic: 6 emails and 3 text messages. Ottu support sets these up, the same way [notification templates](/business/notifications/) work for one-off payments.

There is no catalog to maintain. Your team sets the plan name and price in each checkout call. The [developer docs](/developers/payments/autopay/#api-reference) show the request shape.

## Limits {#limits}

This is the full list. Each limit comes with what to do instead.

- **API-only.** There is no AutoPay screen in the dashboard. *Instead:* ask your developer to integrate it, and try the [live demo](/developers/payments/autopay/#live-demo).
- **Renewals need a gateway with auto-debit.** *Instead:* pick a gateway from the [filtered catalog](/business/payments/gateways/?features=autodebit).
- **Monthly or yearly only.** *Instead:* pick the closer of the two.
- **A running subscription cannot be edited.** Amount, frequency, billing day, retry count, and customer email are fixed. *Instead:* cancel it and create a new one.
- **No pause.** *Instead:* cancel at the end of the paid period, then create a new subscription when the customer returns.
- **Emails switch off only at creation,** and only the three billing-cycle emails. *Instead:* decide when your developer creates the subscription.
- **You cannot pick the exact charge time.** *Instead:* choose the start date. It sets the billing day.
- **No dashboard list of subscriptions.** *Instead:* your developer [lists them through the API](/developers/payments/autopay/#api-reference), filtered by status, for example Past Due.
- **Your team cannot undo a pending cancellation.** *Instead:* send your customer their page link. They can undo it there.

## FAQ {#faq}

<FAQ>
  <FAQItem question="Can I cancel a customer's subscription immediately?">
    Yes, but only your team can, through the API. A cancellation the customer starts always waits for the end of their paid period. See [When a payment fails](#when-a-payment-fails) for Past Due.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can I change the price of a subscription that's already running?">
    No. Cancel it and create a new one. See [Limits](#limits).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can I pause a subscription?">
    No. See [Limits](#limits) for what to do instead.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Which payment gateways and currencies does AutoPay support?">
    See the [gateway catalog, filtered to auto-debit](/business/payments/gateways/?features=autodebit). It lists the gateways and the currencies of each.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Where do I see AutoPay payments?">
    Each billing cycle is one Ottu transaction in [Payment Management](/business/payment-management/), and all retries of that cycle are on it. The first payment is the transaction from your own checkout.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I refund a billing cycle?">
    Find the cycle's transaction in [Payment Management](/business/payment-management/) and refund it, in full or in part. A refund doesn't cancel the subscription. Cancel it separately if the customer is leaving.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What happens when my customer's card expires?">
    The charge fails like any other declined card. Ottu retries and emails the customer. If every attempt fails, the subscription becomes Past Due. The customer can add a new card and pay from their self-service page.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can a customer have more than one card on file?">
    Yes. They can switch the active card from their self-service page at any time.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does AutoPay support Arabic?">
    Yes. Each subscription's emails and texts go out in English or Arabic, chosen when it is created, and the self-service page offers both languages.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What if a customer opens a link that was replaced?">
    The page shows "This link has expired". The customer already received the new link by email and text. You can also send it yourself. See [Regenerating a link revokes the old one](#regenerate-link).
  </FAQItem>
</FAQ>
