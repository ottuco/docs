# AutoPay

AutoPay is Ottu's managed subscriptions feature. You make one [Checkout API](../checkout-api) call with `payment_type: "auto_pay"`, and Ottu runs the rest: each [billing cycle](/glossary/#term-billing-cycle), the retries, the [dunning](/glossary/#term-dunning) emails and your customer's self-service page.

Every call on this page uses the standard [Ottu API key](/developers/getting-started/authentication) and targets <code>{OTTU_CONNECT_BASE_URL}</code>. Swap in your own merchant domain when you integrate. Your integration is six steps: see [Workflow](#workflow). To start, [create a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=create#create-api). The [Live Demo](#live-demo) runs the steps on the sandbox.

Related guide: [AutoPay for merchants](/business/autopay/), with what your customer sees and what it does for the business.

## When to Use

- **Use AutoPay** for monthly or yearly plans when Ottu should run the subscription after one checkout call. Why it helps your business: [Why use AutoPay](/business/autopay/#why-use-autopay).
- **Use [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments)** when your own system must decide when each charge happens, for example installments or on-demand billing. You run the schedule and charge a saved card on demand.


## Setup {#setup}

Before your first call:

1. **An Ottu API key.** See [Authentication](/developers/getting-started/authentication).
2. **AutoPay switched on for your account.** Ottu support switches it on. To check, call `GET /b/api/v1/config/`: the response has `autopay_api_url` only when AutoPay is on ([FAQ](#faq)).
3. **A `pg_code` with auto-debit.** Call [Payment Methods](/developers/payments/payment-methods/) with `auto_debit: true` and use a code it returns ([Limits](#limits)).
4. **Optional: a `webhook_url`** for the first-payment result. See [Webhooks](/developers/webhooks/).

To go live, check the same list on your live account. Also call your own merchant domain instead of `sandbox.ottu.net`. Ask Ottu support to register the email and text templates, your support email and your privacy policy URL ([merchant checklist](/business/autopay/#setting-up-autopay)).


## Guide

### Workflow

<AutoPayStory />

In the diagram and on this page, "you" is the merchant: your server makes the API calls.

Your part, in order:

1. **Before your first call.** Check the [Setup](#setup) list.
2. **Create the subscription.** Call [Create a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=create#create-api): `POST /b/checkout/v1/pymt-txn/` with `payment_type: "auto_pay"` and the `autopay` block. Store `extra.autopay.subscription_id` from the `201` response.
3. **Show the checkout.** Send your customer to `checkout_url` (Ottu's checkout page), or start the [Checkout SDK](/developers/payments/checkout-sdk/) ([Web](/developers/payments/checkout-sdk/web), [iOS](/developers/payments/checkout-sdk/ios), [Android](/developers/payments/checkout-sdk/android) or [Flutter](/developers/payments/checkout-sdk/flutter)) with the `session_id` from the same response to keep them on your own page or app. See [First payment and activation](#first-payment-and-activation). The [Live Demo](#live-demo) runs the SDK. To manage the subscription later, your customer uses their [self-service page](#customer-self-service-page), hosted by Ottu.
4. **Receive the result.** If you set a `webhook_url`, Ottu calls it with the first-payment result. [Verify its signature](/developers/webhooks/verify-signatures#which-signature). Your account's [webhook format](/developers/webhooks/#webhook-formats) sets the payload: [Payment Events](/developers/webhooks/payment-events) for formats 1 and 2, the [event catalogue](/developers/webhooks/event-catalogue) for format 3. In Python, [`ottu-py`](https://github.com/ottuco/ottu-py#webhook-verification) verifies the signature and, on format 3, [reads the event](https://github.com/ottuco/ottu-py#reading-v3-events).
5. **Start the service.** [Retrieve](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) the subscription until `status` is `active` or `trialing`, then give access. Timing: [First payment and activation](#first-payment-and-activation).
6. **Ottu charges every cycle.** Ottu charges and retries. Renewals do not call your `webhook_url`: subscribe a webhook endpoint to [subscription events](#webhook-events) to hear about them. Once a day, [list](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) subscriptions with `status: "past_due"`. [Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) with `mode: "immediate"` when you stop the service.

### Use Cases

After the first payment, Ottu runs the subscription. Each topic below has the rule you code against. The full story is on the business page.

#### First payment and activation {#first-payment-and-activation}

The [Create](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=create#api-reference) response carries `checkout_url` and `session_id`. Your customer pays the first charge on Ottu's checkout page or on your own page, and Ottu saves the card for the later charges. Nothing else changes for the subscription. This is a customer-initiated payment: see [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments) for CIT and MIT.

- **Start state.** A first charge above `0` makes the subscription `active`. A first charge of `0.000` makes it `trialing`.
- **Activation can lag the payment.** `status` stays `pending_setup` until Ottu receives the payment result. If that result is missed, a backup check activates the subscription 20 to about 35 minutes after you created it. Poll; do not hard-code a wait. Your checkout [webhook](/developers/webhooks/) can arrive in that state: treat it as "payment received, activation pending", not as a failure.
- **Poll, do not wait.** [Retrieve](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) the subscription until `status` leaves `pending_setup`. Do not grant access from the webhook's [`autopay` block](/developers/webhooks/payment-events#autopay-block) alone: it is not signed. If the subscription is still `pending_setup` 35 minutes after creation and the payment succeeded, contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.
- **Match the webhook.** In formats 1 and 2, `extra.autopay.subscription_id` in the [payload](/developers/webhooks/payment-events#autopay-block) is the subscription ID you stored at creation. Format 3 payloads are in the [event catalogue](/developers/webhooks/event-catalogue). As a fallback, the webhook's `session_id` equals `setup_session_id` on the subscription.
- **Setup can fail.** The subscription becomes `setup_failed` when the customer cancels the checkout, when 24 hours pass without payment, or when the payment succeeds but Ottu cannot save the card. Ottu does not retry it: create a new subscription. If the first payment succeeded, refund it, because Ottu does not refund it automatically, and contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.

#### Free trials {#free-trials}

Send a checkout `amount` of `0.000`. Your customer pays nothing, Ottu saves the card, and the subscription starts in `trialing`, not `active`. The first real charge comes one period after `start_date`.

#### Billing Cycles {#billing-cycles}

- Ottu creates one cycle at a time: the next cycle exists only after the previous one is paid. Each cycle locks its own `amount_due` when it is created.
- The customer pays the first charge at checkout, on the day they complete it. `start_date` does not change that day. It sets the date of cycle 1 and the billing day for every later cycle.
- When `amount` is above `0`, that charge is cycle 1: `charge_type: "cit"`, `scheduled_date` equal to `start_date`, and already `paid`. Cycle 2 comes one period (month or year) after `start_date`.
- When `amount` is `0.000`, the card is saved only. Cycle 1 is the first real charge, one period after `start_date`.

Billing days and short months: [Anchor days](/business/autopay/#anchor-days).

#### Retries & Dunning {#retries-and-dunning}

Ottu charges a renewal from 00:00 UTC on its billing date. It retries a failed charge one hour apart: 3 retries by default (4 attempts in total), or 1 to 10 with `retry_count` on the `autopay` block. When the last attempt fails, the cycle becomes `failed` and the subscription becomes `past_due`, normally the same day. A slow gateway or an outage can move this later, so run your `past_due` check after that day. Ottu never cancels a `past_due` subscription and does not bill it again: it stays that way until the customer pays or you [cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) it with `mode: "immediate"`. To find these subscriptions, [list](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"`. What your customer gets: [When a payment fails](/business/autopay/#when-a-payment-fails).

#### Notifications {#notifications}

Ottu emails and texts your customer. Two fields control it: `notification_preferences` on the `autopay` block switches off the three billing emails (upcoming charge, payment failed, final failure), and the checkout `language` (default `en`) sets the language of every message and of the customer's page. What each message says and when it goes: [The emails and texts](/business/autopay/#the-emails).

To read it, call [Get the page link](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=page-link#api-reference) and take `page_url`. It is a credential: anyone holding it can act on the subscription. #### Payment outcomes {#payment-outcomes}

How each charge result shows on the attempt, the billing cycle and the subscription. Read them with [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference) and [Retrieve](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference).

##### First payment paid {#outcome-first-payment-paid}

The attempt `result` is `success` and cycle 1 is `paid` with `charge_type: "cit"`. The subscription becomes `active`, or `trialing` after a `0.000` first charge.

##### First payment declined {#outcome-first-payment-declined}

No attempt and no cycle yet. The subscription stays `pending_setup`, and your customer can try again in the checkout.

##### First payment never completes {#outcome-setup-failed}

The payment was canceled, 24 hours passed without payment, or the card was not saved. There is no cycle, and the subscription becomes `setup_failed`.

##### Renewal paid {#outcome-renewal-paid}

The attempt `result` is `success` and the cycle is `paid`. The subscription stays `active`.

##### Renewal declined, retries left {#outcome-renewal-retry}

The attempt `result` is `failed` and the cycle is `retry_scheduled`. The subscription does not change while Ottu retries.

##### Last retry declined {#outcome-last-retry-declined}

The attempt `result` is `failed` and the cycle is `failed`. The subscription becomes `past_due`.

##### Past-due balance paid {#outcome-past-due-paid}

Your customer pays on their self-service page. The attempt `result` is `success`, the cycle is `paid`, and the subscription is `active` again.

##### Result not confirmed {#outcome-needs-triage}

Ottu could not confirm a charge. The attempt `result` is `pending` and the cycle is `needs_triage`. The subscription does not change. Contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.

#### Canceling {#canceling}

There are three ways a cancellation happens. Each one changes the [Subscription object](#subscription-object) and sends a [subscription event](#webhook-events).

##### You cancel now {#cancel-now}

[Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) with `mode: "immediate"`. `status` becomes `canceled` at once, scheduled charges and retries stop, `cancellation.canceled_at` is set and `cancellation.canceled_by.kind` is `api_key`. Nothing is refunded. Ottu sends [`subscription.canceled`](#event-canceled).

##### You or the customer cancel at the end of the period {#cancel-at-period-end}

Cancel with `mode: "at_period_end"`, or your customer cancels on their self-service page. `status` stays as it is, `cancellation.at_period_end` is `true`, `cancellation.cancel_date` is the last paid day and `next_billing_date` is `null`. On the next billing date Ottu cancels instead of charging. Ottu sends [`subscription.cancel_scheduled`](#event-cancel-scheduled), then [`subscription.canceled`](#event-canceled) on that date.

##### The customer undoes it {#cancel-undo}

Before the period ends, your customer presses **Reactivate subscription** on their self-service page. The pending cancellation is cleared and the next charge is scheduled again. Ottu sends [`subscription.cancel_reverted`](#event-cancel-reverted).

- **Only you can cancel immediately.** A customer's cancellation always waits for the end of the paid period.
- **Only the customer can undo a pending cancellation.** There is no API call for it. To keep a customer you canceled, create a new subscription.
- **`at_period_end` is refused while a payment is due or in progress**: on a `past_due` subscription, while a cycle is being retried, or before the first payment. Ottu answers `422`; use `mode: "immediate"` then.
- **Retry safely.** Cancel requires an `Idempotency-Key` header. Create one UUID per cancel and store it with your cancel record, so a retry after a timeout sends the same key.

#### Webhook events {#webhook-events}

Renewals do not call your `webhook_url`. To hear about them, add a webhook endpoint subscribed to `subscription.*` events: see [Subscription events](/developers/webhooks/event-catalogue#subscription-events) for the setup, the envelope and the signature.

##### `subscription.created` {#event-created}

**Sent when:** Your checkout call created the subscription (`pending_setup`).

**What to do:** Link it to your record. Do not grant access yet.

##### `subscription.activated` {#event-activated}

**Sent when:** Ottu confirmed the first payment: the subscription is `active`, or `trialing` after a `0.000` first charge.

**What to do:** Grant access.

##### `subscription.setup_failed` {#event-setup-failed}

**Sent when:** The payment was canceled, 24 hours passed without payment, or the card could not be saved.

**What to do:** Create a new subscription. Refund the first payment if it went through: Ottu does not.

##### `subscription.renewed` {#event-renewed}

**Sent when:** A billing cycle was paid: a renewal, the first real charge after a trial, or a `past_due` balance your customer paid.

**What to do:** Extend access to the next billing date.

##### `subscription.payment_failed` {#event-payment-failed}

**Sent when:** One charge attempt on a billing cycle failed. Ottu sends it for every attempt.

**What to do:** Nothing yet: Ottu retries. You can tell your customer.

##### `subscription.past_due` {#event-past-due}

**Sent when:** The last retry failed.

**What to do:** Apply your `past_due` policy: ask your customer to pay, or cancel with `mode: "immediate"`.

##### `subscription.card_updated` {#event-card-updated}

**Sent when:** Your customer changed the card on their self-service page.

**What to do:** Nothing. Retrieve the subscription if you show the card.

##### `subscription.cancel_scheduled` {#event-cancel-scheduled}

**Sent when:** You or your customer canceled at the end of the period.

**What to do:** Keep access until `cancellation.cancel_date`.

##### `subscription.cancel_reverted` {#event-cancel-reverted}

**Sent when:** Your customer undid a pending cancellation.

**What to do:** Keep access. Billing continues.

##### `subscription.canceled` {#event-canceled}

**Sent when:** Billing stopped: an immediate cancel, or a scheduled one that reached its date.

**What to do:** End access.

##### `subscription.expired` {#event-expired}

**Sent when:** The last billing cycle was paid. There are no more charges.

**What to do:** End access, or offer a new subscription.

**Keep access in sync.** Handle every event the same way:

1. Verify the `Ottu-Signature` on the raw body and skip an event `id` you have already processed ([Verify signatures](/developers/webhooks/verify-signatures#which-signature)).
2. Find your record by `data.subscription_id`.
3. [Retrieve](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) the subscription and act on its current `status` (see [Status](#subscription-states)). Events can arrive out of order, so the event name tells you to look, and the subscription tells you the state.
4. Keep the daily [list](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) of `past_due` subscriptions as a backstop, in case a delivery fails for good.

### Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

#### Create returns `400` with the error under `autopay` {#trouble-create-400-autopay}

**Why:** `customer_email` is missing. It is required, and its error is reported under the `autopay` key.

**Fix:** Send `customer_email` and check it first. More in [Errors](#error-autopay-rejected).

#### Still `pending_setup` after the customer paid {#trouble-stuck-pending-setup}

**Why:** Ottu activates the subscription when it receives the payment result. If that result is missed, a backup check activates it 20 to about 35 minutes after creation.

**Fix:** Keep polling. If it is still `pending_setup` 35 minutes after creation and the payment succeeded, contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.

#### `setup_failed` but the customer paid {#trouble-setup-failed-paid}

**Why:** The card could not be saved.

**Fix:** Refund the payment (Ottu does not) and create a new subscription.

#### No webhook for a renewal {#trouble-no-renewal-webhook}

**Why:** Renewals never call your `webhook_url`.

**Fix:** Subscribe a webhook endpoint to [subscription events](#webhook-events), or read [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference).

#### The SDK payload says `auto_debit`, not `auto_pay` {#trouble-sdk-auto-debit}

**Why:** Renewals are ordinary auto-debit payments, so the [Checkout SDK](../checkout-sdk/web) preload carries `auto_debit` and no `autopay` key.

**Fix:** Nothing. The Checkout API response still reports `auto_pay`.

#### A cycle has `status: "needs_triage"` {#trouble-needs-triage}

**Why:** Ottu could not confirm the result of a charge.

**Fix:** Contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.

#### Another error status {#trouble-other-errors}

**Why:** Each status has its own cause.

**Fix:** See [Errors](#errors): one entry per error, with its body and fix.

Every error answers with a JSON body; the `detail` text can differ from the examples. A rule error has `detail` (for a person) and `code` (for your code): branch on `code`. A field error names the field and lists what is wrong with it. Each body below is a real sandbox response, and each error has its own link. The endpoint tabs show each status with its example, and the Checkout API's own errors are in [Error codes](/developers/reference/error-codes).

##### `400` AutoPay rejected the create {#error-autopay-rejected}

```json
{"autopay": ["AutoPayClient rejected request (422)"]}
```

**When:** Create, when AutoPay refuses the subscription. Most often `customer_email` is missing.

**Fix:** Send `customer_email`, then check the `autopay` block against the Create tab.

##### `400` Idempotency-Key missing {#error-idempotency-key-missing}

```json
{"Idempotency-Key": ["This header is required."]}
```

**When:** Cancel or Regenerate without the header.

**Fix:** Send a UUID v4 in `Idempotency-Key`.

##### `400` invalid_idempotency_key {#error-invalid-idempotency-key}

```json
{"detail": "Idempotency-Key must be a valid UUID.", "code": "invalid_idempotency_key"}
```

**When:** The header is not a UUID.

**Fix:** Send a UUID v4.

##### `400` Invalid mode {#error-invalid-mode}

```json
{"mode": ["\"later\" is not a valid choice."]}
```

**When:** Cancel with a `mode` other than `immediate` or `at_period_end`.

**Fix:** Fix the field named in the body.

##### `400` Invalid limit {#error-invalid-limit}

```json
{"limit": ["Ensure this value is greater than or equal to 1."]}
```

**When:** List with `limit` outside 1 to 100.

**Fix:** Fix the field named in the body.

##### `401` Not authenticated {#error-not-authenticated}

```json
{"detail": "Authentication credentials were not provided."}
```

**When:** The API key is missing or wrong.

**Fix:** Send `Authorization: Api-Key {your_api_key}` ([Authentication](/developers/getting-started/authentication)).

##### `404` subscription_not_found {#error-subscription-not-found}

```json
{"detail": "Not found", "code": "subscription_not_found"}
```

**When:** No subscription with this `subscription_id` on your account.

**Fix:** Check the ID you stored at creation.

##### `405` Method not allowed {#error-method-not-allowed}

```json
{"detail": "Method \"GET\" not allowed."}
```

**When:** `GET` on List subscriptions.

**Fix:** List is a `POST`, and so is following its `next` link.

##### `409` idempotency_conflict {#error-idempotency-conflict}

```json
{"detail": "Idempotency key … was already used with a different request payload.", "code": "idempotency_conflict"}
```

**When:** A cancel reused a key with another body or another subscription.

**Fix:** A new cancel gets a new key. Reuse a key only to retry the same request.

##### `422` invalid_transition {#error-invalid-transition}

```json
{"detail": "Cannot schedule a cancel before setup completes; …", "code": "invalid_transition"}
```

**When:** `mode: "at_period_end"` before the first payment, on a `past_due` subscription, or while a charge is being retried.

**Fix:** Cancel with `mode: "immediate"`.

##### `422` Invalid status filter {#error-invalid-status-filter}

```json
{"detail": [{"type": "enum", "loc": ["body", "payload", "status"], "msg": "Input should be 'pending_setup', …"}]}
```

**When:** List with an unknown `status` value.

**Fix:** Use one of the seven [status values](#subscription-states).

#### Paging and retries {#paging-and-retries}

- **Paging.** List subscriptions and List billing cycles return `count`, `next`, `previous` and `results`. On List subscriptions, `limit` is 1 to 100 (20 by default) and `offset` starts at 0. Follow `next` until it is `null`: with `POST` on List subscriptions, with `GET` on List billing cycles. A `next` link carries `limit` and `offset` in its query string, which win over the body; resend your filters in the body to keep them.
- **Retries.** Cancel and Regenerate need an `Idempotency-Key` header with a UUID v4. A retried cancel with the same key and body returns the first response; the same key with another body returns `409`. A cancel that failed with a `4xx` is not stored, so its retry runs again. Regenerate always issues a new link, even with the same key: before retrying it, compare `page_url` from [Get the page link](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=page-link#api-reference).
- **Create is not idempotent.** Each Create call makes a new subscription. Guard against double submits on your side.

## Limits {#limits}

- **Gateways.** A payment gateway without auto-debit cannot renew a subscription. `pg_codes` must name a gateway that supports tokenization and auto-debit. See the [gateways with auto-debit](/business/payments/gateways/?features=autodebit). To list the ones enabled on your account, call the [Payment Methods API](/developers/payments/payment-methods/) with `auto_debit: true`.
- **Billing periods.** `autopay.frequency` is `monthly` or `yearly`. There is no weekly or custom interval.
- **No edits.** A running subscription cannot be edited: amount, `autopay.frequency`, billing day, retry count, `language`, `notification_preferences` or customer email. [Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) it and create a new one. There is no proration.
- **Retries.** You set only the retry count, and only when you create the subscription. Ottu sets the interval. See [Retries & Dunning](#retries-and-dunning).
- **No `past_due` call to your `webhook_url`.** A `past_due` subscription is not billed again until the customer pays or you cancel it (see [Retries & Dunning](#retries-and-dunning)). Ottu does not call your `webhook_url` for it. A webhook endpoint subscribed to `subscription.*` events gets [`subscription.past_due`](#webhook-events). Without one, poll [List subscriptions](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"`.
- **No dashboard screen.** AutoPay is API-only. Renewal charges appear in Payment Management as normal transactions.
- **Sandbox.** You cannot trigger a renewal early.

## Best Practices

- **Guard against double submits.** Each create call makes a new subscription.
- **Cancel needs a UUID `Idempotency-Key`.** A retried cancel with the same key replays safely. Regenerate needs no key. Before retrying a timed-out regenerate, call `page-link` and compare `page_url`.
- **Get `page_url` from `page-link`.** Read it from `page-link` at any time after create. Use regenerate only to revoke a link.
- **Know that regenerate contacts the customer.** Ottu emails and texts the new link to your customer, and the old link stops working. Still deliver `page_url` yourself where your product needs it.
- **Always send `customer_email`.** It is required. A missing one returns a `400` with the error under the `autopay` key, which looks like a problem with the autopay block. Check `customer_email` first.
- **Expect `auto_debit` in the SDK payload.** The [Checkout SDK](../checkout-sdk/web) preload payload for an `auto_pay` session carries `payment_type: "auto_debit"` and no `autopay` key, because Ottu charges every renewal as an ordinary auto-debit payment. The Checkout API response still reports `auto_pay`.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <FAQItem question="What's the real difference between AutoPay and auto-debit?">
    Who owns the schedule. With auto-debit, your system decides when to charge the saved token, and you own retries and dunning. With AutoPay, you make one checkout call and Ottu owns the schedule, retries, dunning and the customer page. See [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments) for the auto-debit side.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Why is List Subscriptions a POST instead of a GET?">
    The list endpoint takes its filters in a request body, and it only reads. Keep `customer_id` in that body: a customer identifier in a URL ends up in access logs.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I know when the customer finishes the first payment?">
    Poll [Retrieve a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) and check `status`. It leaves `pending_setup` when Ottu confirms the first payment. If your account has a webhook endpoint set up by Ottu support, Ottu also sends `subscription.activated` when the subscription turns active or starts a trial. The checkout [webhook](/developers/webhooks/) is only "payment received". Timing and details: [First payment and activation](#first-payment-and-activation).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What happens when the customer's card expires?">
    Ottu doesn't track the card's expiry date, so there is no warning in advance. The next renewal fails and follows the normal path: retries, then `past_due` and the final email. The customer adds a new card and pays the balance on the [self-service page](#customer-self-service-page).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can I change the amount of a running subscription?">
    No. A running subscription cannot be edited (see [Limits](#limits)). [Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) the subscription with `mode: "at_period_end"` so the paid period runs out, then have the customer start a new one through checkout with the new `autopay.recurring_amount`. There is no proration.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I test a failed renewal?">
    The sandbox [test cards](/developers/payments/ottu-sandbox#test-cards) include three Visa cards that decline renewals: `411111111111114`, `411111111111115` and `411111111111116`. They save normally on the first payment and decline on every later merchant-initiated charge. Create the subscription with `pg_codes: ["ottu-sandbox-kwd"]` (the Ottu Sandbox gateway, KWD) and pay the first charge with one of them. Other sandbox gateways don't recognise these cards. The sandbox has no way to trigger a renewal early: the first renewal is charged one billing period after `start_date`. When it declines, follow the cycle in [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference): it moves through `retry_scheduled` to `failed`, and the subscription becomes `past_due`. Every scenario is in [Testing](#testing).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does Ottu send a webhook when a renewal is charged?">
    Not to your `webhook_url`. Only the first payment, made through your checkout session, calls your [`webhook_url`](/developers/webhooks/). Ottu charges renewals itself. If your account has a webhook endpoint set up by Ottu support, Ottu sends `subscription.renewed` to that endpoint when a renewal is paid, and `subscription.payment_failed` or `subscription.past_due` when it fails. You can also read the outcome from [Retrieve a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) (`current_cycle`) or [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference), and poll [List subscriptions](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"` to catch failed ones.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I check that AutoPay is enabled on my account?">
    Call `GET /b/api/v1/config/`. The response includes `autopay_api_url` when AutoPay is enabled, and leaves the key out entirely when it is not. Test for the key, not its value.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Where do I see AutoPay charges in the dashboard?">
    In Payment Management, as normal transactions. Each billing cycle is one transaction with order number `<subscription ID>-c<cycle number>` (for example `sub_…-c2`) and payment type auto-debit, and every retry attempt sits on that one transaction. The first payment is your own checkout transaction. A refund on a renewal is a normal [refund](/business/payment-management/) of that transaction, and it does not cancel the subscription.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQ>
