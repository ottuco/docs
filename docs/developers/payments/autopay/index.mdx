# AutoPay

AutoPay is Ottu's managed subscriptions feature. You make one [Checkout API](../checkout-api) call with `payment_type: "auto_pay"`, and Ottu runs the rest: each [billing cycle](/glossary/#term-billing-cycle), the retries, the [dunning](/glossary/#term-dunning) emails and your customer's self-service page.

Every call on this page uses the standard [Ottu API key](/developers/getting-started/authentication) and targets <code>{OTTU_CONNECT_BASE_URL}</code>. Swap in your own merchant domain when you integrate. Start with the [Live Demo](#live-demo): it shows each call and its response.

Related guide: [AutoPay for merchants](/business/autopay/), with what your customer sees and what it does for the business.

:::tip Boost Your Integration
Ottu offers SDKs and tools to speed up your integration. See [Getting Started](../../getting-started/#boost-your-integration) for all available options.
:::


## When to Use

- **Use AutoPay** for monthly or yearly plans when Ottu should run the subscription after one checkout call. You build no billing scheduler, no retry logic and no customer page.
- **Use [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments)** when your own system must decide when each charge happens, for example installments or on-demand billing. You run the schedule and charge a saved card on demand.


## Guide

### Workflow

<AutoPayStory />

In the diagram and on this page, "you" is the merchant: your server makes the API calls.

Create the subscription with one [Checkout API](../checkout-api) call. Ottu sends the result of the first payment to your `webhook_url`, if you set one. Its payload is a payment webhook: see [Payment Events](/developers/webhooks/payment-events) for formats 1 and 2, or the [event catalogue](/developers/webhooks/event-catalogue) for format 3, and [verify its signature](/developers/webhooks/verify-signatures#which-signature). That URL is not called for a renewal, so read a renewal from the API: [retrieve](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) the subscription, read its [billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference), or [list](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) subscriptions with `status: "past_due"`.

### Use Cases

After the first payment, Ottu runs the subscription. Each topic below has the rule you code against. The full story is on the business page.

#### First payment and activation {#first-payment-and-activation}

<Beside image="/img/developers/autopay/first-charge-checkout.png" alt="Ottu's checkout page for the first AutoPay charge, with the card form and the amount due">

Your customer pays the first charge on the checkout page, and Ottu saves the card for the later charges. This is a customer-initiated payment: see [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments) for CIT and MIT.

</Beside>

- **Start state.** A first charge above `0` makes the subscription `active`. A first charge of `0.000` makes it `trialing`.
- **Activation can lag the payment.** `status` stays `pending_setup` until Ottu receives the payment result. If that result is missed, a backup check activates the subscription within about 15 minutes of the payment, and never earlier than 20 minutes after you created it. Your checkout webhook usually arrives in that state: treat it as "payment received, activation pending", not as a failure.
- **Poll, do not wait.** [Retrieve](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) the subscription until `status` leaves `pending_setup`. Do not grant access from the webhook's [`autopay` block](/developers/webhooks/payment-events#autopay-block) alone: it is not signed. If the subscription is still `pending_setup` well after a successful payment, contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.
- **Match the webhook.** `extra.autopay.subscription_id` in the payload is the subscription ID you stored at creation. As a fallback, the webhook's `session_id` equals `setup_session_id` on the subscription.
- **Setup can fail.** The subscription becomes `setup_failed` when the customer cancels the checkout, when 24 hours pass without payment, or when the payment succeeds but Ottu cannot save the card. Ottu does not retry it: create a new subscription. If the first payment succeeded, refund it, because Ottu does not refund it automatically, and contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.

#### Free trials {#free-trials}

Send a checkout `amount` of `0.000`. Your customer pays nothing, Ottu saves the card, and the subscription starts in `trialing`, not `active`. The first real charge comes one period after `start_date`.

#### Billing Cycles {#billing-cycles}

- Ottu creates one cycle at a time: the next cycle exists only after the previous one is paid. Each cycle locks its own `amount_due` when it is created.
- `start_date` does not delay the checkout charge. The customer pays it when they complete checkout.
- When `amount` is above `0`, that charge is cycle 1: `charge_type: "cit"`, `scheduled_date` equal to `start_date`, and already `paid`. Cycle 2 comes one period (month or year) after `start_date`.
- When `amount` is `0.000`, the card is saved only. Cycle 1 is the first real charge, one period after `start_date`.

Billing days and short months: [Anchor days](/business/autopay/#anchor-days).

#### Retries & Dunning {#retries-and-dunning}

Ottu retries a failed charge one hour apart: 3 retries by default (4 attempts in total), or 1 to 10 with `retry_count` on the `autopay` block. When the last attempt fails, the cycle becomes `failed` and the subscription becomes `past_due`. Ottu never cancels a `past_due` subscription and does not bill it again: it stays that way until the customer pays or you [cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) it with `mode: "immediate"`. To find these subscriptions, [list](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"`. What your customer gets: [When a payment fails](/business/autopay/#when-a-payment-fails).

#### Notifications {#notifications}

Ottu emails and texts your customer. Two fields control it: `notification_preferences` on the `autopay` block switches off the three billing emails (upcoming charge, payment failed, final failure), and the checkout `language` (default `en`) sets the language of every message and of the customer's page. What each message says and when it goes: [The emails and texts](/business/autopay/#the-emails).

#### Customer Self-Service Page {#customer-self-service-page}

<Beside image="/img/developers/autopay/self-service-page.png" alt="The customer's self-service page: subscription summary, saved card and payment history">

Read `page_url` from [Get the page link](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=page-link#api-reference). It is a credential: anyone holding it can act on the subscription. What your customer can do there: [The self-service page](/business/autopay/#the-self-service-page). The rules for a customer's cancellation are in [Cancel a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference).

</Beside>

## Limits {#limits}

- **Gateways.** A payment gateway without auto-debit cannot renew a subscription. `pg_codes` must name a gateway that supports tokenization and auto-debit. See the [gateways with auto-debit](/business/payments/gateways/?features=autodebit). To list the ones enabled on your account, call the [Payment Methods API](/developers/payments/payment-methods/) with `auto_debit: true`.
- **Billing periods.** `autopay.frequency` is `monthly` or `yearly`. There is no weekly or custom interval.
- **No edits.** A running subscription cannot be edited: amount, `autopay.frequency`, billing day, retry count, `language`, `notification_preferences` or customer email. [Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) it and create a new one. There is no proration.
- **Retries.** You set only the retry count, and only when you create the subscription. Ottu sets the interval. See [Retries & Dunning](#retries-and-dunning).
- **No `past_due` alert.** A `past_due` subscription is not billed again until the customer pays or you cancel it (see [Retries & Dunning](#retries-and-dunning)). Ottu does not notify you, so poll [List subscriptions](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"`.
- **No dashboard screen.** AutoPay is API-only. Renewal charges appear in Payment Management as normal transactions.
- **Sandbox.** You cannot trigger a renewal early.

## Best Practices

- **Send a UUID `Idempotency-Key` on cancel.** A retried cancel with the same key replays safely. Regenerate needs no key. Before retrying a timed-out regenerate, call `page-link` and compare `page_url`.
- **Get `page_url` from `page-link`.** Read it from `page-link` at any time after create. Use regenerate only to revoke a link.
- **Know that regenerate contacts the customer.** Ottu emails and texts the new link to your customer, and the old link stops working. Still deliver `page_url` yourself where your product needs it.
- **Always send `customer_email`.** It is required. A missing one returns a `400` with the error under the `autopay` key, which looks like a problem with the autopay block. Check `customer_email` first.
- **Expect `auto_debit` in the SDK payload.** The [Checkout SDK](../checkout-sdk/web) preload payload for an `auto_pay` session carries `payment_type: "auto_debit"` and no `autopay` key, because Ottu charges every renewal as an ordinary auto-debit payment. The Checkout API response still reports `auto_pay`.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <FAQItem question="What's the real difference between AutoPay and auto-debit?">
    Who owns the schedule. With auto-debit, your system decides when to charge the saved token, and you own retries and dunning. With AutoPay, you make one checkout call and Ottu owns the schedule, retries, dunning and the customer page. See [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments) for the auto-debit side.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Why is List Subscriptions a POST instead of a GET?">
    The list endpoint takes its filters in a request body, and it only reads. Keep `customer_id` in that body: a customer identifier in a URL ends up in access logs.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I know when the customer finishes the first payment?">
    Poll [Retrieve a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) and check `status`. It leaves `pending_setup` when Ottu confirms the first payment. The checkout webhook is only "payment received". Timing and details: [First payment and activation](#first-payment-and-activation).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What happens when the customer's card expires?">
    Ottu doesn't track the card's expiry date, so there is no warning in advance. The next renewal fails and follows the normal path: retries, then `past_due` and the final email. The customer adds a new card and pays the balance on the [self-service page](#customer-self-service-page).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can I change the amount of a running subscription?">
    No. A running subscription cannot be edited (see [Limits](#limits)). [Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) the subscription with `mode: "at_period_end"` so the paid period runs out, then have the customer start a new one through checkout with the new `autopay.recurring_amount`. There is no proration.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I test a failed renewal?">
    The sandbox [test cards](/developers/payments/ottu-sandbox#test-cards) include three Visa cards that decline renewals: `411111111111114`, `411111111111115` and `411111111111116`. They save normally on the first payment and decline on every later merchant-initiated charge. Create the subscription with `pg_codes: ["ottu-sandbox-kwd"]` (the Ottu Sandbox gateway, KWD) and pay the first charge with one of them. Other sandbox gateways don't recognise these cards. The sandbox has no way to trigger a renewal early: the first renewal is charged one billing period after `start_date`. When it declines, follow the cycle in [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference): it moves through `retry_scheduled` to `failed`, and the subscription becomes `past_due`.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does Ottu send a webhook when a renewal is charged?">
    No. Only the first payment, made through your checkout session, calls your `webhook_url`. Ottu charges renewals itself, and no webhook reaches you for a renewal or its retries. Read the outcome from [Retrieve a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) (`current_cycle`) or [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference), and poll [List subscriptions](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"` to catch failed ones.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I check that AutoPay is enabled on my account?">
    Call `GET /b/api/v1/config/`. The response includes `autopay_api_url` when AutoPay is enabled, and leaves the key out entirely when it is not. Test for the key, not its value.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Where do I see AutoPay charges in the dashboard?">
    In Payment Management, as normal transactions. Each billing cycle is one transaction with order number `<subscription ID>-c<cycle number>` (for example `sub_…-c2`) and payment type auto-debit, and every retry attempt sits on that one transaction. The first payment is your own checkout transaction. A refund on a renewal is a normal [refund](/business/payment-management/) of that transaction, and it does not cancel the subscription.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQ>
