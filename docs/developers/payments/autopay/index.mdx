# AutoPay

AutoPay is Ottu's managed subscriptions feature. You make one [Checkout API](../checkout-api) call with `payment_type: "auto_pay"`. Ottu then generates each [billing cycle](/glossary/#term-billing-cycle), retries failed charges, sends the [dunning](/glossary/#term-dunning) emails and hosts a self-service page for your customer. Want to see the result first? [See what your customers see](/business/autopay/) on the business page.

Every call on this page uses the standard [Ottu API key](/developers/getting-started/authentication), with no separate credential. Start with the [Live Demo](#live-demo): it shows each call and its response.

:::note Base URL
Every API call in this guide targets <code>{OTTU_CONNECT_BASE_URL}</code>. Swap in your own merchant domain when you integrate.
:::


## When to Use

Use AutoPay for monthly or yearly plans when you want Ottu to run the subscription after one checkout call:

- **No billing scheduler to build.** Pricing and schedule are fields on your checkout call. There is no plan catalog and no "create a plan" step.
- **Dunning is automatic.** Ottu retries failed charges, moves the subscription to `past_due` and emails the customer.
- **Self-service is included.** A hosted page lets your customer see status, pay an outstanding balance, update the card or cancel.

:::tip Choosing between AutoPay and Auto-Debit
Want to decide exactly when each charge happens, for example installments or on-demand billing? Run the schedule yourself with [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments) and charge a saved card on demand.
:::

:::tip Boost Your Integration
Ottu offers SDKs and tools to speed up your integration. See [Getting Started](../../getting-started/#boost-your-integration) for all available options.
:::

### Use Cases

After the first payment, Ottu runs the subscription. These are the behaviours you can rely on.

#### First payment and activation {#first-payment-and-activation}

<Beside image="/img/developers/autopay/first-charge-checkout.png" alt="Ottu's checkout page for the first AutoPay charge, with the card form and the amount due">

The first payment is a **CIT** (customer-initiated transaction): your customer pays on the checkout page, and Ottu saves their card so it can charge the later cycles without them. See [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments) for the CIT/MIT background.

</Beside>

**Activation can lag the payment.** Your customer can pay while the subscription still shows `pending_setup`. Ottu activates the subscription when it receives the payment result. If that result is missed, a backup check activates it within about 15 minutes of the payment, and never earlier than 20 minutes after you created the subscription. Until then:

- `status` is `pending_setup`, and your checkout webhook usually arrives in that state. Treat it as "payment received, activation pending", not as a failure.
- Do not hard-code a wait. [Retrieve](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) the subscription and poll until `status` leaves `pending_setup`. Do not grant access from the webhook's `autopay` block alone: it is not signed. See [The `autopay` block](/developers/webhooks/payment-events#autopay-block).
- If it is still `pending_setup` well after a successful payment, contact Ottu support with the subscription ID.

:::warning The first charge amount decides the start state
A first charge above `0` makes the subscription `active`. A first charge of `0.000` makes it `trialing`. See [Free trials](#free-trials).
:::

If the payment succeeds but Ottu cannot save the card, the subscription becomes `setup_failed`. Create a new subscription and contact Ottu support with the subscription ID. If the customer never pays, the subscription becomes `setup_failed` after 24 hours, or at once if they cancel the checkout. Ottu does not retry it; create a new subscription.

**Match the webhook to the subscription.** Read `extra.autopay.subscription_id` in the webhook payload: it is the subscription ID you stored when you created the subscription. As a fallback, the webhook's `session_id` equals `setup_session_id` on the subscription.

#### Subscription States {#subscription-states}

A subscription is always in one of seven states. Click a state in the diagram, or read the list below it.

<AutoPayStates audience="developer" />

##### `pending_setup` {#state-pending-setup}

Created by the checkout call; waiting for the first payment. **What to do:** do not grant access yet. See [First payment and activation](#first-payment-and-activation).

##### `trialing` {#state-trialing}

The first payment settled with a `0.000` amount. No money moved, but the schedule is live. **What to do:** grant access. See [Free trials](#free-trials).

##### `active` {#state-active}

Billing normally. **What to do:** grant access. Nothing else is needed.

##### `past_due` {#state-past-due}

A billing cycle used up every retry. **What to do:** ask the customer to pay on their page, or cancel with `mode: "immediate"`. See [Retries & Dunning](#retries-and-dunning).

##### `canceled` {#state-canceled}

Terminal. No more charges. **What to do:** end access.

##### `expired` {#state-expired}

Terminal. The last billing cycle was paid and the next one would fall after the subscription's `end_date`. **What to do:** end access, or create a new subscription to continue.

##### `setup_failed` {#state-setup-failed}

The customer canceled the checkout, 24 hours passed without payment, or the first payment succeeded but the card was not saved. **What to do:** create a new subscription. Ottu does not retry it. If the first payment succeeded, refund it. Ottu does not refund it automatically.

See [Lifecycle and billing cycles](/business/autopay/#lifecycle-and-billing-cycles) for the business view.

#### Free trials {#free-trials}

Send a checkout `amount` of `0.000` to start with a free period. Your customer pays nothing, Ottu only saves the card, and the subscription starts in `trialing`, not `active`. The first real charge comes one period after `start_date`.

#### Billing Cycles {#billing-cycles}

Cycles are generated one at a time, so the next one isn't created until the previous one is paid. Each cycle locks its own `amount_due` when it's created. Billing is anchor-day clamped: a subscription anchored on the 31st charges on the 28th (or 29th) in February, then returns to the 31st. See [Lifecycle and billing cycles](/business/autopay/#lifecycle-and-billing-cycles).

`start_date` does not delay the checkout charge. The customer pays it on the checkout page, whenever they complete checkout.

##### When `amount` is greater than `0` {#cycles-paid-first-charge}

The customer pays `amount` at checkout. That charge is cycle 1, with `charge_type: "cit"`, `scheduled_date` set to `start_date`, and already `paid`. Cycle 2 comes one period (month or year) after `start_date`.

##### When `amount` is `0.000` {#cycles-free-trial}

The customer pays nothing and the card is saved only. Cycle 1 is the first real charge, one period after `start_date`. Cycle 2 is the period after that.

#### Retries & Dunning {#retries-and-dunning}

A failed charge is retried one hour apart. The default is 3 retries, 4 attempts in total, and you can set it from 1 to 10 with `retry_count` on the `autopay` block. When the last attempt fails, the cycle becomes `failed`, the subscription becomes `past_due`, and the customer gets a final email with a link to pay the balance. See [When a payment fails](/business/autopay/#when-a-payment-fails).

Read `past_due` from the API: [list subscriptions](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"` and poll that list. A `past_due` subscription is not billed again.

:::warning Ottu never auto-cancels a past-due subscription
A `past_due` subscription stays that way until the customer pays or you cancel it with `mode: "immediate"`.
:::

#### Notifications {#notifications}

Ottu sends your customer six emails and three text messages (SMS). Three emails belong to the billing cycle: an upcoming-charge reminder, a payment-failed notice after each failed attempt while retries remain, and a final-failure email when they run out. Monthly subscriptions get one reminder, about 3 days before the charge. Yearly ones get three, about 31, 11 and 4 days before. You can switch these three off per subscription with `notification_preferences` on the `autopay` block.

The other three emails cannot be switched off: subscription created, setup completed and link replaced. Each of the three also sends an SMS when a phone number is on file. The language comes from the checkout `language` field when you create the subscription (default `en`). It drives the customer page and every customer email. See [The emails](/business/autopay/#the-emails).

#### Customer Self-Service Page {#customer-self-service-page}

<Beside image="/img/developers/autopay/self-service-page.png" alt="The customer's self-service page: subscription summary, saved card and payment history">

Read `page_url` from the [page-link endpoint](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=page-link#api-reference). It is a credential: anyone holding it can act on the subscription. Your customer can view status and billing history, add or switch a card, pay an outstanding balance, and cancel or undo a pending cancellation. See [The self-service page](/business/autopay/#the-self-service-page).

</Beside>

:::note Customer cancellation is end-of-period
A cancellation from the self-service page takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Only the merchant cancel call with `mode: "immediate"` stops a subscription right away. The customer can undo a pending cancellation while the subscription is `active` or `trialing`.

Ottu refuses an end-of-period cancel while the subscription is `past_due` or a charge is outstanding. The customer's page asks them to pay the balance first. Your `mode: "at_period_end"` call gets `422 invalid_transition`. Cancel with `mode: "immediate"` instead; Ottu allows it from `past_due`.
:::

### Error contract {#error-contract}

These are the statuses you will meet most often, so you can handle them without parsing message text. Each tab in the [API Reference](#api-reference) above lists every status code its endpoint returns. On the subscription tabs, pick a status in the response panel to see an example body.

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | Ottu rejected the request as malformed, for example a `limit` above 100 on list, an unknown cancel `mode`, or a missing `Idempotency-Key` on cancel or regenerate. | Fix the request. Retrying unchanged will fail again. |
| `401` | Not authenticated. | Check your [API key](/developers/getting-started/authentication). |
| `404` | No such subscription for your merchant. | Do not retry. |
| `409` | `Idempotency-Key` was already used on `cancel` with a different request body (`code: idempotency_conflict`). | Send a new key for a new request. Reuse a key only to retry the exact same request. |
| `422` | Ottu rejected the request. Either a value is invalid (for example an unknown `status` filter on list, where `detail` lists each invalid field), or the subscription is in a state that does not allow it, such as canceling one that has already ended (`code: invalid_transition`). | Fix the request, or re-read the subscription's `status`. Retrying unchanged will fail again. |
| `423` | The Ottu installation is paused (`code: service_paused`). All six subscription endpoints return it. | Retry later. |
| `503` | The subscription service is unavailable. | Retry with backoff. |
| `504` | The subscription service timed out. The request may still have been applied. | Retry a cancel with the same `Idempotency-Key`. Before retrying anything else, retrieve the subscription to see whether it changed. |

## Limits {#limits}

- **Gateways.** A payment gateway without auto-debit cannot renew a subscription. `pg_codes` must name a gateway that supports tokenization and auto-debit. See the [gateways with auto-debit](/business/payments/gateways/?features=autodebit). To list the ones enabled on your account, call the [Payment Methods API](/developers/payments/payment-methods/) with `auto_debit: true`.
- **Billing periods.** `autopay.frequency` is `monthly` or `yearly`. There is no weekly or custom interval.
- **No edits.** A running subscription cannot be edited: amount, `autopay.frequency`, billing day, retry count, `language`, `notification_preferences` or customer email. [Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) it and create a new one. There is no proration.
- **Retries.** You set only the retry count, and only when you create the subscription. Ottu sets the interval. See [Retries & Dunning](#retries-and-dunning).
- **No `past_due` alert.** A `past_due` subscription is not billed again until the customer pays or you cancel it (see [Retries & Dunning](#retries-and-dunning)). Ottu does not notify you, so poll [List subscriptions](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"`.
- **No dashboard screen.** AutoPay is API-only. Renewal charges appear in Payment Management as normal transactions.
- **Sandbox.** You cannot trigger a renewal early.

## Best Practices

- **Send a UUID `Idempotency-Key` on cancel.** A retried cancel with the same key replays safely. Regenerate needs no key. Before retrying a timed-out regenerate, call `page-link` and compare `page_url`.
- **Get `page_url` from `page-link`.** Read it from `page-link` at any time after create. Use regenerate only to revoke a link.
- **Know that regenerate contacts the customer.** Ottu emails and texts the new link to your customer, and the old link stops working. Still deliver `page_url` yourself where your product needs it.
- **Always send `customer_email`.** It is required. A missing one returns a `400` with the error under the `autopay` key, which looks like a problem with the autopay block. Check `customer_email` first.
- **Expect `auto_debit` in the SDK payload.** The [Checkout SDK](../checkout-sdk/web) preload payload for an `auto_pay` session carries `payment_type: "auto_debit"` and no `autopay` key, because Ottu charges every renewal as an ordinary auto-debit payment. The Checkout API response still reports `auto_pay`.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <FAQItem question="What's the real difference between AutoPay and auto-debit?">
    Who owns the schedule. With auto-debit, your system decides when to charge the saved token, and you own retries and dunning. With AutoPay, you make one checkout call and Ottu owns the schedule, retries, dunning and the customer page. See [Recurring Payments & Auto-Debit](/developers/cards-and-tokens/recurring-payments) for the auto-debit side.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Why is List Subscriptions a POST instead of a GET?">
    The list endpoint takes its filters in a request body, and it only reads. Keep `customer_id` in that body: a customer identifier in a URL ends up in access logs.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I know when the customer finishes the first payment?">
    Poll [Retrieve a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) and check `status`. It leaves `pending_setup` when Ottu confirms the first payment. The checkout webhook is only "payment received". Timing and details: [First payment and activation](#first-payment-and-activation).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What happens when the customer's card expires?">
    Ottu doesn't track the card's expiry date, so there is no warning in advance. The next renewal fails and follows the normal path: retries, then `past_due` and the final email. The customer adds a new card and pays the balance on the [self-service page](#customer-self-service-page).
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can I change the amount of a running subscription?">
    No. A running subscription cannot be edited (see [Limits](#limits)). [Cancel](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cancel#api-reference) the subscription with `mode: "at_period_end"` so the paid period runs out, then have the customer start a new one through checkout with the new `autopay.recurring_amount`. There is no proration.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I test a failed renewal?">
    The sandbox [test cards](/developers/payments/ottu-sandbox#test-cards) include three Visa cards that decline renewals: `411111111111114`, `411111111111115` and `411111111111116`. They save normally on the first payment and decline on every later merchant-initiated charge. Create the subscription with `pg_codes: ["ottu-sandbox-kwd"]` (the Ottu Sandbox gateway, KWD) and pay the first charge with one of them. Other sandbox gateways don't recognise these cards. The sandbox has no way to trigger a renewal early: the first renewal is charged one billing period after `start_date`. When it declines, follow the cycle in [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference): it moves through `retry_scheduled` to `failed`, and the subscription becomes `past_due`.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does Ottu send a webhook when a renewal is charged?">
    No. Only the first payment, made through your checkout session, calls your `webhook_url`. Ottu charges renewals itself, and no webhook reaches you for a renewal or its retries. Read the outcome from [Retrieve a subscription](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=retrieve#api-reference) (`current_cycle`) or [Billing cycles](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=cycles#api-reference), and poll [List subscriptions](/developers/payments/autopay/?autopay-api=list#api-reference) with `status: "past_due"` to catch failed ones.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How do I check that AutoPay is enabled on my account?">
    Call `GET /b/api/v1/config/`. The response includes `autopay_api_url` when AutoPay is enabled, and leaves the key out entirely when it is not. Test for the key, not its value.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Where do I see AutoPay charges in the dashboard?">
    In Payment Management, as normal transactions. Each billing cycle is one transaction with order number `<subscription ID>-c<cycle number>` (for example `sub_…-c2`) and payment type auto-debit, and every retry attempt sits on that one transaction. The first payment is your own checkout transaction. A refund on a renewal is a normal [refund](/business/payment-management/) of that transaction, and it does not cancel the subscription.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQ>
