# Webhooks v1 and v2

New integrations use [webhook format 3](/developers/webhooks/event-catalogue). The pages below describe formats 1 and 2, which Ottu still sends to accounts that use them. Ottu never moves an account to a new format on its own.

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Payment Events (formats 1 and 2)](/developers/webhooks/payment-events) | The notification Ottu sends when a payment is processed: its fields, setup on the payment and the redirect behaviour of `200` and `201`. |
| [Operation Events (formats 1 and 2)](/developers/webhooks/operation-events) | The notification Ottu sends after a refund, capture or void, including the pending `refund_queued` state. |

## Coming from v1 or v2?

Format 3 sends every webhook as an event, with the payment itself in `data`, and renames two values: `refund_queued` is `refund_pending`, and `invalided` is `invalidated`. [Here is how to migrate](/developers/webhooks/migrate-to-v3): a field-by-field map from formats 1 and 2, and steps you can test before anything changes for your customers.
